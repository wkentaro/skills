# Keep Review Policy outside review-fix

The review-and-fix skill accepts a caller's free-form Review Brief and resolves it into a caller-owned Review Policy, asking when routing is ambiguous instead of selecting or broadening reviews silently. It owns one bounded round of non-delegating review, evidence verification, repair, and checks; callers own repeated rounds, reviewer models, Git history, and publication because the former fixed roster hid fan-out and coupled orchestration to particular review types.
